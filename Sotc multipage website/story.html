The story of Shadow Of The Colossus

The story follows the journey of young man named Wander who travels to the forbidden land. Wander on horseback, sets off to the forbidden lands, seeking to bring back a woman named Mono. In order to bring Mono back Wander must slay 16 colossus, all of which guard a piece of a god's soul.
